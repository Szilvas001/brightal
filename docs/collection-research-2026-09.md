Design direction: warm gold, substantial rounded shoulders, low integrated bezels, small flush-looking diamonds on wide cigar bands, gentle fluting, controlled negative space and connected stacked rows. Pinterest is visual reference, not proof of 2026 popularity; dated editorial reporting supplies trend context. Celebrity references establish an aesthetic direction, not an endorsement or claim that a celebrity wore these original BRIGHTAL models. No logos, proprietary CAD or product images are copied into the shop.

Geometry direction: shared millimetre-based parametric construction for preview and export, nominal inner circumference, closed connected metal body, Boolean-cut stone cavities and access bores, explicit stone dimensions and a stone schedule. A successful geometric check is distinct from approval for casting and setting; actual stones, alloy/process allowances and workshop approval remain necessary.
